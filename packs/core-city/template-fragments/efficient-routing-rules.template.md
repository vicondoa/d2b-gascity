## Routing rules

Use the official Gas City roles and formulas. Create or inspect the governing
bead, then dispatch the formula or named role that owns the work.

Model tiers select providers for configured roles. Gas City may show provider
profiles as implicit agents, but they are not the workflow roles the mayor
dispatches and must not be used as bead routing metadata. Do not create
replacement agents when an imported role already owns the task.
